# Hero Background Video Requirements

The hero background video should embody the premium, sophisticated nature of DriftiX as a luxury delivery platform. Here are the detailed specifications:

## Technical Requirements
- Format: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- Duration: 15-20 seconds (looped)
- Frame rate: 24-30fps (cinematic)
- File size: < 8MB (optimized for web)
- Bitrate: ~3-4 Mbps
- Audio: Optional soft instrumental loop (future enhancement)

## Visual Style
- Premium, cinematic quality
- Deep purple/blue brand-tinted overlay
- Slow, subtle motion for mobile optimization
- Smooth transitions between scenes
- Professional lighting and composition
- High-end product focus

## Scene Requirements
The video should showcase all key service offerings with the following scenes:

### Rideshare Scene
- Clean, luxury vehicle pulling up
- Subtle light reflections on car surface
- Professional driver interaction
- Smooth camera movement

### Food Delivery Scene
- Chef plating food in slow motion
- High-end restaurant kitchen setting
- Professional food presentation
- Warm, inviting lighting

### Smoke Shop Scene
- Classy close-up of premium vapes/glass
- Well-lit display case
- Professional product arrangement
- Subtle smoke effects (optional)

### Alcohol Delivery Scene
- Ice dropping into whiskey glass
- Bar shelf zoom with premium bottles
- Chilled drinks condensation
- Elegant glassware

### Grocery Scene
- Fresh produce on conveyor
- Professional bagging process
- High-quality product close-ups
- Clean, organized environment

## Scene Transitions
- Smooth crossfades between scenes
- Consistent color grading
- Maintain brand color palette
- Professional pacing

## Usage
The video will be used as a background for the hero section with:
- Dark overlay (opacity: 0.7)
- Gradient overlays matching brand colors
- Content layered on top
- Responsive scaling for all devices
